FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about JOWEFCO Technical Services, our welding and fabrication work, custom projects, quotes, location, materials, and timelines.

What services does JOWEFCO Technical Services provide?

JOWEFCO provides welding, metal fabrication, structural steelwork, custom metalwork, and technical services. We serve residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and Nigeria. Our five core services are: Welding Services, Metal Fabrication, Structural Steelwork, Custom Metal Doors & Security, and Technical Services.

Does JOWEFCO handle custom projects?

Yes. We provide custom solutions based on project requirements, measurements, designs, and specifications. Bring your drawings, sketches, or just an idea — our engineering team will work with you to deliver a finished product. Custom work is a significant part of what we do, and our workshop in Port Harcourt is set up to handle one-off projects as well as repeat production runs.

How can I request a quote?

Contact JOWEFCO Technical Services through our phone, WhatsApp, or the project booking form. Tell us about your project, share dimensions or drawings, and we will prepare a detailed quote and timeline. We typically respond within one business day. For urgent enquiries, WhatsApp is usually the fastest channel.

Where is JOWEFCO located?

Our workshop is located in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We serve clients in Port Harcourt and surrounding areas including Eleme, Obio-Akpor, Elelenwo, Oyigbo, and the wider South-South region. For larger industrial projects, we can mobilize to sites across Nigeria. View our location on the map.

What materials do you work with?

We work with mild steel, stainless steel, galvanized steel, aluminum, and selected alloys. We use high-tensile steel and premium materials to ensure maximum durability and long-term reliability for every project. For structural work, we source certified steel sections and verify mill certificates for grade and yield strength.

Do you offer on-site welding and installation?

Yes. Our mobile team offers on-site welding, fabrication repairs, and installation across Port Harcourt and Rivers State. For larger industrial projects, we can mobilize to sites across Nigeria. Our mobile welding unit is equipped for workshop-grade work in the field, which is especially useful for equipment repairs and structural modifications where transporting the work to our workshop is not practical.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines depend on scope, material availability, and site access. Small custom metalwork can be completed within 1 to 2 weeks, while large structural steel projects may take 4 to 8 weeks or more. We provide a clear timeline with every quote, and we stick to it. If you have a deadline, tell us upfront so we can plan accordingly.

Do you provide drawings or do I need to bring my own?

We can work either way. If you have engineering drawings or sketches, we will fabricate exactly to those specifications. If you don't have drawings, our team can prepare fabrication drawings based on your description, measurements, and requirements. For structural steelwork, we usually work from your structural engineer's drawings to ensure full compliance with the design intent.

Do you handle both small and large projects?

Yes. We handle everything from a single custom bracket or door to multi-tonne structural steel installations for warehouses and industrial facilities. The same standard of workmanship applies regardless of project size — small jobs are not treated as less important than large ones.

What are your working hours?

Our standard working hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM (West African Time). For urgent industrial breakdowns, we can arrange after-hours response on request. Contact us via WhatsApp outside working hours and we will respond as soon as we are back online.

How do I pay for a project?

For most projects, we require an initial deposit to cover materials, with the balance due on completion and handover. Payment terms are clearly stated in every quote. For larger projects, we may agree a milestone-based payment schedule. We accept bank transfers and other payment methods commonly used in Nigeria.

Do you offer guarantees on your work?

Yes. We stand behind our workmanship. If any issue arises from our fabrication or installation within a reasonable period after handover, we will return to address it. The specific warranty terms depend on the type of work and are documented in the project quote. Material warranties (e.g. on galvanizing or paint finishes) are passed through from the material supplier where applicable.

Can you work with my architect or structural engineer?

Absolutely. We routinely collaborate with architects, structural engineers, contractors, and project managers. We are happy to review drawings, suggest fabrication-friendly details, attend site meetings, and coordinate with other trades. Many of our long-term clients are professionals who value a fabrication partner that understands engineering drawings and construction sequences.

Do you sell ready-made products or only custom work?

Both. Our online shop features a selection of ready-made metal products available for purchase. We also take on fully custom projects where every detail is built to specification. If you don't see what you need in the shop, contact us — we can almost certainly make it.

I'm not sure what service I need. Can you advise?

Of course. Many clients come to us with a problem but aren't sure of the best technical solution. Contact us with a description of what you want to achieve, and our team will advise on the most appropriate service, materials, and approach. There is no charge for initial consultations and quotes.

Still have questions?

Our team in Port Harcourt is ready to help with any enquiry about welding, fabrication, structural steelwork, or technical services in Nigeria.
