Overview

JOWEFCO Technical Services is a trusted provider of structural steelwork in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and across Nigeria. We design, fabricate, and install the load-bearing steel components that form the skeleton of warehouses, factories, commercial buildings, mezzanine floors, and industrial structures. Every structural component we produce is fabricated to engineering specifications and inspected before it leaves the workshop.

Structural steel is unforgiving — mistakes in dimension, weld quality, or material grade can have serious consequences. That is why we follow documented procedures on every structural project, work only with certified materials, and coordinate closely with your structural engineer or architect to ensure full compliance with the design intent.

Structural Steel Products We Fabricate

Our structural steelwork portfolio covers the full range of components used in Nigerian construction and industrial projects:

  • Universal Beams (UB) & Universal Columns (UC). Standard I-section beams and columns cut to length, drilled, and prepared for connection.
  • Roof Trusses & Girders. Fabricated lattice trusses and plate girders for warehouse roofs, factory buildings, and large-span structures.
  • Mezzanine Floors. Steel-framed intermediate floors including joists, decking supports, and stair access.
  • Staircases & Handrails. Industrial and commercial steel staircases with matching handrails and balustrades.
  • Support Structures. Equipment supports, pipe racks, platforms, and walkways for industrial facilities.
  • Portal Frames. Pre-fabricated portal frame components for warehouse and factory construction.
  • Connection Plates & Cleats. Custom-fabricated connection details to engineer specifications.

Our Structural Steel Process

Structural steelwork demands a more rigorous process than general fabrication because the consequences of error are higher. Our process is designed to match engineering standards:

  • 1. Design Review. We review your engineer's drawings, flag any fabrication concerns, and confirm material specifications.
  • 2. Fabrication Drawings. We produce detailed shop drawings showing every cut, hole, and weld required.
  • 3. Material Verification. We source certified steel sections and verify mill certificates for grade and yield strength.
  • 4. Fabrication. Cutting, drilling, welding, and assembly in our workshop under controlled conditions.
  • 5. Inspection & Marking. Every component is inspected for dimensions and weld quality, then marked for installation sequence.
  • 6. Delivery & Erection. We transport to site and erect the steelwork, including bolting, final welding, and alignment.

Sectors We Serve

Our structural steelwork has been used across a wide range of sectors in Nigeria:

  • Commercial buildings — office blocks, retail centres, and mixed-use developments.
  • Industrial facilities — warehouses, factories, processing plants, and workshops.
  • Oil & gas support — pipe racks, equipment platforms, and access structures.
  • Religious & community buildings — churches, halls, and event centres with large clear spans.
  • Residential — custom steel staircases, mezzanines, and architectural features for high-end homes.
