Overview

JOWEFCO Technical Services provides a broad range of technical and engineering services that go beyond pure welding and fabrication. We support residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and Nigeria with repairs, installation, maintenance, and on-site engineering support. Our team combines fabrication skills with mechanical and maintenance expertise, which means we can handle projects that require both structural work and equipment integration.

Many of our industrial clients rely on us as their go-to technical partner — the first call when something breaks, when new equipment arrives, or when a planned maintenance shutdown needs execution. We respond quickly, work safely, and document what we do.

Technical Services We Offer

Our technical services cover the practical engineering needs that keep homes, businesses, and industrial facilities running:

  • Industrial Repairs. Welding and fabrication repairs for machinery, conveyors, structures, and equipment that has failed or worn out.
  • Equipment Installation. Installation of newly purchased equipment including fabrication of supports, brackets, and foundations as required.
  • Plant Maintenance. Scheduled and reactive maintenance of plant equipment, structures, and access systems.
  • On-Site Engineering Support. Engineering presence on your site for projects, shutdowns, or ongoing support contracts.
  • Structural Modifications. Modifications to existing structures including openings, reinforcements, and extensions.
  • Metal Restoration. Restoration of rusted, corroded, or damaged metal structures including surface preparation and refinishing.
  • Custom Brackets & Supports. Fabrication of bespoke brackets, mounts, and supports for equipment, signage, and utilities.
  • Site Welding. Mobile welding services for repairs and modifications on site, across Port Harcourt and Rivers State.

Sectors We Serve

Our technical services team supports clients across multiple sectors in Nigeria:

  • Manufacturing plants requiring ongoing equipment maintenance and structural repairs.
  • Oil and gas support facilities needing on-site welding and structural modifications.
  • Commercial properties requiring security upgrades, structural modifications, and maintenance.
  • Residential clients needing repairs, modifications, or new installations.
  • Religious and community buildings requiring structural additions or modifications.
  • Property developers requiring technical support during construction or handover phases.

How We Approach Technical Work

Technical services work demands flexibility — every job is different. But we apply the same disciplined approach regardless of the project size:

  • 1. Rapid Response. When you call us, we respond quickly. For urgent breakdowns we aim to be on site within hours, not days.
  • 2. Assessment. We assess the problem, propose a solution, and confirm scope and cost before starting work.
  • 3. Execution. We carry out the work safely, using appropriate procedures and materials, with progress updates on longer jobs.
  • 4. Documentation. We document what we did, what materials we used, and any recommendations for future maintenance or follow-up.
  • 5. Follow-Up. We check back after the work is complete to ensure the fix is holding and the client is satisfied.
