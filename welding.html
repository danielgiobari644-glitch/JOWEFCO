Overview

JOWEFCO Technical Services provides professional welding solutions for clients across Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and the wider Nigerian market. Our welders are certified in MIG, TIG, and arc welding processes, and we routinely work with mild steel, stainless steel, galvanized steel, and selected alloys. Whether you need a small repair weld on a piece of equipment or kilometres of structural welds on a new build, our workshop and mobile teams have the capacity and the experience to deliver.

We follow industry-recognised welding procedures (WPS) on every project, and we test critical welded joints for soundness before sign-off. This commitment to procedure is what separates professional welding from amateur work — and it is the reason our structural welds continue to perform years after installation.

Our Welding Capabilities

We maintain a fully equipped welding bay in our Port Harcourt workshop and a mobile welding unit for on-site work across Rivers State. Our capabilities include:

  • MIG Welding (GMAW). High-deposition welding for structural steel, frames, and thick-section fabrication. Ideal for production work and large components.
  • TIG Welding (GTAW). Precision welding for stainless steel, aluminium, and thin-gauge materials where appearance and joint quality matter most.
  • Arc / Stick Welding (SMAW). Versatile welding for on-site repairs, outdoor work, and rusty or dirty materials where other processes struggle.
  • Structural Welding. Certified welding for load-bearing beams, columns, trusses, and connections on buildings and industrial structures.
  • Pipe Welding. Welding of process piping, pressure-rated lines, and utility pipes for industrial facilities.
  • On-Site Welding. Mobile welding team that brings workshop-grade equipment to your site for repairs, modifications, and installations.

Materials We Weld

Our welders are qualified to work on a wide range of ferrous and non-ferrous materials commonly used in Nigerian construction and industrial projects. We select the correct filler metal, shielding gas, and procedure for each material to ensure full-strength, defect-free joints.

  • Mild Steel. The workhorse of Nigerian construction. We weld it daily for beams, gates, doors, frames, and general fabrication.
  • Stainless Steel. For food-grade, chemical, and architectural applications where corrosion resistance is essential.
  • Galvanized Steel. Commonly used for outdoor structures. We follow correct procedures to manage zinc fumes and joint quality.
  • Aluminium. Lightweight applications including frames, housings, and decorative work.
  • Cast Iron (Repairs). Specialised repair welding for broken cast-iron components and machinery parts.

Typical Welding Projects We Handle

Our welding services span small residential jobs to large industrial installations. Some typical projects include:

  • Welding of structural steel beams and columns for commercial buildings.
  • Fabrication and welding of security gates, doors, and window bars for homes and businesses.
  • On-site welding repairs for industrial machinery, conveyors, and process equipment.
  • Welding of pipe spools and process piping for manufacturing plants.
  • Welding of staircases, handrails, and mezzanine floors for warehouses.
  • Custom welding of brackets, mounts, and supports for equipment installation.
