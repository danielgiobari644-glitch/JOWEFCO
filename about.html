About Us

Engineering Excellence Since 2011

JOWEFCO Technical Services is a professional technical services company in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria, specializing in precision welding, metal fabrication, structural steelwork, and engineering solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

Our Company Story

JOWEFCO Technical Services was founded in 2011 with a clear mission: to deliver professional welding, fabrication, and engineering services that meet international standards while remaining accessible to Nigerian residential, commercial, and industrial clients. What started as a small welding workshop in Port Harcourt has grown into a full-service technical services company serving clients across Rivers State and the wider South-South region.

Over the past 15+ years, we have completed hundreds of projects ranging from small custom metalwork pieces for individual homeowners to large structural steel installations for industrial facilities. Our growth has been driven by word-of-mouth referrals from satisfied clients who value our commitment to material integrity, dimensional accuracy, and on-time delivery. We have invested continuously in modern equipment and ongoing training for our welders and fabricators, ensuring we can handle today's most demanding engineering challenges.

Today, JOWEFCO operates a fully equipped workshop in Port Harcourt with capabilities in MIG, TIG, and arc welding, CNC cutting, sheet metal forming, and heavy structural assembly. Our mobile deployment team also brings workshop-grade welding and fabrication directly to client sites across Rivers State and beyond, reducing project lead times and transportation costs for our customers.

Our Experience

JOWEFCO has delivered projects across multiple sectors, giving us broad practical experience that informs every new engagement. Our portfolio includes:

  • Structural steelwork for warehouses, factories, and commercial buildings, including beams, columns, trusses, and mezzanine floors.
  • Custom metal fabrication for residential clients, including gates, doors, railings, fences, staircases, and architectural features.
  • Industrial welding and repairs for manufacturing plants, oil and gas support facilities, and process equipment.
  • Security solutions, including burglar doors, window bars, automated sliding gates, and perimeter fencing for homes and businesses.
  • Custom metalwork for retail and hospitality, including display fixtures, bespoke furniture, and artistic installations.

Our team brings together certified welders, experienced fabricators, and engineering technicians who collaborate on every project. This multidisciplinary approach means we can take on complex briefs that require both structural calculations and aesthetic refinement — all under one roof.

Our Values

Every project at JOWEFCO is guided by four core values that define how we work and what our clients can expect from us. These values are not just marketing language — they are the operating principles that have sustained our business for over a decade.

Quality Workmanship

We use high-tensile steel, premium alloys, and certified welding procedures on every project. Quality is non-negotiable, regardless of project size or budget.

On-Time Delivery

We provide clear timelines with every quote and stick to them. Our project planning and resource allocation are designed to meet deadlines without compromising quality.

Client-Focused Approach

We work directly with each client from initial consultation to final installation. Your specifications, budget, and timeline shape every decision we make.

Material Integrity

We source materials from trusted suppliers and verify every batch. We never substitute materials without informing the client and documenting the change.

Our Professional Approach

Every JOWEFCO project follows a structured, professional approach designed to eliminate surprises and deliver predictable results. Whether the project is a single custom door or a multi-tonne structural steel installation, the same disciplined process applies:

  1. Consultation & site assessment. We meet with you to understand the scope, take measurements, and assess site conditions that may affect the work.
  2. Technical drawings & quote. We prepare detailed drawings (or work from yours) and provide an itemized quote with materials, labour, timeline, and total cost.
  3. Material sourcing & fabrication. Once approved, we source materials and begin fabrication in our workshop, with progress updates at key milestones.
  4. Quality inspection. Every fabricated component is inspected against the drawings and welded joints are checked for structural soundness before leaving the workshop.
  5. Delivery & installation. We transport the finished work to your site and handle installation, including any on-site welding, anchoring, or finishing required.
  6. Handover & after-sales support. We walk you through the finished project, address any snags immediately, and remain available for future maintenance or modifications.

This approach has earned JOWEFCO a reputation as one of Port Harcourt's most reliable welding and fabrication companies. We are committed to maintaining that reputation on every project, large or small.
