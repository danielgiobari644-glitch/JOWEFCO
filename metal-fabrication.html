Overview

JOWEFCO Technical Services is a full-service metal fabrication company based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State. We turn raw steel, stainless steel, and aluminium into finished structures and components — whether you arrive with detailed engineering drawings or just a sketch on paper. Our workshop is equipped for cutting, forming, welding, and finishing, allowing us to handle most fabrication projects end-to-end without outsourcing.

We work with homeowners, contractors, architects, and industrial facility managers. The scope of work varies enormously — from a single custom gate to several tonnes of structural steelwork — but the standard of workmanship does not.

Our Fabrication Capabilities

Our Port Harcourt workshop is set up to handle the full range of fabrication tasks required for residential, commercial, and industrial projects:

  • Cutting. Band saw cutting, angle grinding, and plasma cutting for plates, sections, and pipes.
  • Forming & Bending. Press braking and rolling for sheet metal, plus angle bending for frames and rings.
  • Welding & Assembly. MIG, TIG, and arc welding to assemble fabricated components into finished products.
  • Drilling & Machining. Drilling, tapping, and light machining for holes, brackets, and connection details.
  • Finishing. Grinding, deburring, wire-brushing, and surface preparation ready for paint or galvanizing.
  • Installation. On-site installation including anchoring, bolting, and final welding where required.

Common Products We Fabricate

Over the past 15+ years we have fabricated an enormous range of products. Some of the most common include:

  • Custom gates — sliding, swing, pedestrian, and automated gates for residential and commercial properties.
  • Security doors and burglar doors built to specific sizes and reinforcement requirements.
  • Window bars, railings, and balustrades for homes, offices, and industrial staircases.
  • Structural steelwork including beams, columns, trusses, and mezzanine floors.
  • Frames and supports for equipment, signage, and architectural features.
  • Custom furniture and retail display fixtures in steel and stainless steel.
  • Tanks, hoppers, and bins for industrial material handling.
  • Platforms, walkways, and access stairs for industrial facilities.

Our Fabrication Process

Every fabrication project at JOWEFCO follows a clear, documented process designed to eliminate surprises and deliver predictable results:

  • 1. Consultation & Measurement. We discuss your requirements, take site measurements, and review any drawings or sketches you have.
  • 2. Detailed Quote & Drawings. We prepare fabrication drawings (if you don't have them) and an itemised quote covering materials, labour, finishing, and timeline.
  • 3. Material Sourcing. Once approved, we source verified materials from trusted suppliers — we never substitute without informing you.
  • 4. Fabrication. We cut, form, weld, and finish in our workshop, with progress updates at key milestones.
  • 5. Quality Inspection. Every component is checked against the drawings for dimensions, squareness, and weld quality before leaving the workshop.
  • 6. Delivery & Installation. We transport to site and handle installation, including any on-site welding or anchoring required.

Why Choose JOWEFCO for Fabrication?

Choosing JOWEFCO means choosing a fabricator who can handle the full project lifecycle under one roof. You don't have to coordinate a separate designer, a separate workshop, and a separate installer — we do it all, and we take full responsibility for the result.

Our 15+ years of fabrication experience in Port Harcourt means we know what works in Nigerian conditions: which finishes survive the climate, which connections hold up over time, and which details look good and perform well. That experience is reflected in every product we deliver.
