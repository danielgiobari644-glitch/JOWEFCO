Custom Metal Doors & Security Solutions in Nigeria

Overview

JOWEFCO Technical Services designs and manufactures custom metal doors and security solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Port Harcourt, Rivers State, and Nigeria. Our doors and gates are built to specific sizes and reinforcement requirements — we do not sell generic off-the-shelf products. Every door we make is engineered for its opening, its threat level, and its intended use.

Security is one of the top concerns for Nigerian homes and businesses, and a properly built metal door or gate is one of the most effective deterrents against forced entry. Our doors are constructed from heavy-gauge steel, reinforced at stress points, and fitted with high-quality locks and hinges designed to resist attack.

Products We Build

Our custom metal door and security product range includes:

  • Security / Burglar Doors. Heavy-gauge steel doors with reinforced frames, multi-point locks, and anti-pry edges for high-risk entry points.
  • Custom Entry Doors. Aesthetic steel entrance doors for homes and offices that combine security with architectural appeal.
  • Automated Sliding Gates. Motorised sliding gates with remote control, safety sensors, and robust steel construction.
  • Swing Gates. Manual or automated swing gates in single or double-leaf configurations for driveways and entrances.
  • Pedestrian Gates. Smaller gates for pedestrian access, often paired with larger vehicle gates.
  • Window Bars & Grilles. Custom-fit window bars and decorative grilles to deter break-ins while allowing ventilation.
  • Railings & Balustrades. Steel railings for staircases, balconies, and walkways, in both functional and decorative styles.
  • Perimeter Fencing. Steel palisade and panel fencing for properties requiring high-security boundaries.

Standard Features & Options

Every JOWEFCO security product is built to a high standard. The following features are standard across most of our door and gate range, with optional upgrades available:

  • Heavy-gauge steel construction (typically 1.5mm to 3mm sheet depending on application).
  • Reinforced internal frame at hinge and lock points to resist forced entry.
  • Anti-pry edges and reinforced lock pockets on security doors.
  • Multi-point locking systems on request for added security.
  • Powder-coated or painted finish in a colour of your choice.
  • Stainless steel construction option for corrosive environments or premium appearance.
  • Decorative cut-outs, scrolls, and pattern work for architectural applications.
  • Automation packages for gates including motors, remotes, and backup batteries.

Our Door & Gate Process

Because every door and gate we make is custom-built to its opening, our process is designed to ensure accurate measurements and a perfect fit every time:

  • 1. Site Measurement. We visit your site to take precise measurements of the opening and assess surrounding structure.
  • 2. Design & Quote. We propose a design, reinforcement specification, finish, and lock package, with an itemised quote.
  • 3. Fabrication. We build the door or gate in our workshop including frame, sheet, hinges, locks, and any decorative work.
  • 4. Finishing. We apply your chosen finish — paint, powder coating, or galvanizing for outdoor products.
  • 5. Delivery & Installation. We transport to site, install the door or gate, fit hardware, and test operation.
  • 6. Handover. We demonstrate operation, hand over keys / remotes, and address any final adjustments.

Why Choose JOWEFCO for Doors & Security?

Security products only work if they are built correctly. A poorly-built security door can be defeated in seconds; a properly-built one can resist sustained attack. The difference is in the details — gauge of steel, reinforcement at stress points, quality of locks and hinges, and accuracy of fit. We pay attention to all of these on every product we make.

When you buy a door or gate from JOWEFCO, you are buying more than a piece of metal — you are buying the peace of mind that comes from a product engineered to do its job. Contact us to discuss your security requirements and we will design a solution that fits your opening, your threat level, and your budget.
