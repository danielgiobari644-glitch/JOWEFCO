Bank Transfer Settings

These details are shown to customers when they need to make payment. After agreement on WhatsApp, the customer receives these bank transfer details. They send the payment receipt in the same WhatsApp chat for verification.

How customers pay

  1. Customer clicks Order on WhatsApp on a product page.
  2. You discuss the order in WhatsApp and agree the final price.
  3. You share the bank details above with the customer in the same chat.
  4. Customer makes the bank transfer and sends the payment receipt in the chat.
  5. You verify the receipt, then in the Orders tab open the order and click Confirm Payment.
  6. You schedule delivery (date / time / address / instructions) and update the order status through to Delivered.

WhatsApp Ordering Settings

This is the number customers reach when they click 'Order on WhatsApp'. Use international format (country code + number, no + or spaces).

Business & Workshop Settings

Used by the 'Get Directions' button on the product page. Enter decimal degrees (e.g., 4.8156 for Port Harcourt). You can find these by right-clicking your workshop on Google Maps → 'What's here?'.

Fallback used if lat/lng not set.

Where these are used

The workshop coordinates power the Get Directions to Workshop button on product pages. If you move locations in the future, just update these fields — no code changes needed.

Logo & Branding

Upload your JOWEFCO logo to any image host (e.g. Imgur, Cloudinary, your own server) and paste the direct URL below. The logo will be used in the website header, footer, admin login, browser favicon, and social share previews. Leave the field empty to use the default logo.jpg bundled with the site.

Recommended: PNG or JPG, max ~600px wide, transparent or white background. Firebase Storage is intentionally NOT used — paste any hosted URL.

Enter a URL and click Preview

Where the logo appears

  • Website header — top-left of every public page
  • Website footer — bottom of every public page (shown in white)
  • Admin login screen — centered above the sign-in form
  • Admin sidebar — top of the admin dashboard sidebar
  • Browser tab icon — favicon for all pages
  • Mobile home-screen icon — apple-touch-icon when bookmarked
  • Social share preview — og:image meta tag used when the site is shared on Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp, etc.
