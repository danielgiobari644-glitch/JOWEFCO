Hero Background Media

Add image or video URLs for the homepage background. Multiple items will create a slideshow.

How to get image/video URLs:
  • Imgur: Upload → Right-click image → "Copy image address"
  • Google Drive: Upload → Share → "Anyone with link" → Use: https://drive.google.com/uc?id=FILE_ID
  • Dropbox: Upload → Share → Copy link → Change dl=0 to dl=1
  • YouTube: Not supported. Use direct MP4 URLs only.
